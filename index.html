About

Xiaolei Min is an Associate Professor and PhD Supervisor at the School of Design, Hunan University, China, and a researcher at the Key Laboratory of Digital Cultural and Creative Intelligent Design Technologies, Ministry of Culture and Tourism of China.

With over a decade of fieldwork experience in ethnic minority regions across western China, she integrates design research, digital technologies, and local cultural knowledge to explore new approaches to cultural innovation and heritage sustainability.

Grounded in interdisciplinary research and design practice, she seeks to advance digital cultural experiences, strengthen living heritage ecosystems, and explore the role of design in facilitating social and cultural transitions towards more sustainable and inclusive futures.
